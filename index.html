Why are our streams delayed?

📡 Terrestrial / Cable
Direct antenna & traditional cable have the lowest latency (~3 to 7s real delay).
🛰️ Satellite TV
Signal bounces to orbit and back, introducing roughly 5-10s of broadcast delay.
📱 Official OTT Apps
HLS / DASH video chunking causes 20-40s delay compared to physical TV.
🌐 Unofficial Streams
Re-encoded web streams typically lag behind by 45s to 90s+. Highest spoiler risk!
